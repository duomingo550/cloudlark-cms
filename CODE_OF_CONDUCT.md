# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Disagreement is welcome; personal
attacks, harassment, discrimination, and disclosure of private information are
not.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project
spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior is harmful. Report urgent concerns privately through the repository's
security contact channel.
